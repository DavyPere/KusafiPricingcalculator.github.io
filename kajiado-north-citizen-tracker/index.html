WHAT DOES THE RESEARCH SHOW?

The gap between approval and proof.

An approved budget is not the same as a completed building. A launch photo is not proof that a facility is working. Here is what we can — and cannot — currently confirm from the documents in our register.

Read this first

These are gaps in our records, not proof that projects failed. A project may have been finished without a close-out document appearing in our research. We invite the relevant offices to provide updates.

01 / APPROVED, DELIVERY NOT YET CHECKED
43 / 57

43 current-term entries have no confirmed completion record in our research.

We found approved project entries from FY2022/23 and FY2023/24. We still need contracts, payment records, inspection reports or recent on-site evidence to establish what happened next.

02 / PROJECTS STARTED EARLIER
32

Entries that began before the current MP's term.

We mark older projects separately so completed work is credited fairly. A later ribbon-cutting is not proof that the entire project began under the present office-holder.

03 / AUDIT CLAIMS TO CHECK
3

Audit findings still need the original pages checked.

They remain visible for research transparency, but should not be treated as established facts until the underlying Auditor-General text is verified.

04 / WHY YOU CAN TRUST THE PROCESS

Our method.

A public record is only useful when readers can see where information came from — and what remains unknown.

01

Document the claim

Start from a budget, approved NG-CDF proposal, official implementation record, government publication or dated public statement.

02

Check what it means

Separate approval, allocation, disbursement, procurement, construction, commissioning and actual operation. A stage score is not a physical inspection.

03

Verify independently

Seek photos with dates, site visits, contracts, completion certificates and official responses. Protect people and private information during verification.

04

Publish fairly

Identify the responsible agency; provide a right of reply; show disputed or incomplete facts clearly; retain updates and corrections.

Important distinction: Roads, drainage and water works may be managed by county government, KeRRA, KURA or other agencies rather than the NG-CDF. An MP appearing at a launch is not evidence of project funding or ownership.
